# Example: fictional v2 redesign

> Fully fictional firm. Not a real business.

**Firm:** Northbridge Sample Data Co. (invented)
**Ask:** Fix the blog on mobile, then review the whole site and propose v2.

## Issues found (fictional)

- Blog pages about 2.5x wider than a phone screen; menu unreachable
- Home headline did not say what the firm sells or who it is for
- Contact buried among eight equal nav links
- Gold-on-cream text failed contrast
- Missing meta descriptions and social preview tags

## v2 changes (fictional)

- Hero: "Business registry data for researchers, with a documented source."
- "Talk to us" button in the header on every page
- Trust strip: founded 2012, sample city
- Blog reflowed; menu works at 390px and 1366px
- Meta, canonical, and Organization JSON-LD added

## Handoff

Delivered `site-v2.zip` with screenshots. Listed two open factual questions for the owner. No deploy until they said go.
